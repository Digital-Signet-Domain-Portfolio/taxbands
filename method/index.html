Direct answer

We cite. We date. We update. Every numeric claim on taxbands.co.uk carries an inline source tick that links to the primary source. The ledger below lists every cited source with its retrieval date29. The updated log records every edit by date.

The convention

  • SourceTick – a small superscript that links to a primary source (HMRC, VOA, OBR, SAA, Scottish Government, Welsh Government, HMT, LGA, IFS, MHCLG).
  • VintageStamp – every page with figures carries a "rates verified DD MMM YYYY · next refresh after [event]" stamp.
  • Updated log – public changelog at /updated records each numeric edit by date.

How the calculator is tested

The UnifiedBandResolver engine (src/lib/calculate.ts) computes income tax band-by-band against HMRC's published worked examples. The £100k taper is unit-tested at salaries £99,999 / £100,001 / £110,000 / £125,140 / £125,141 to confirm the discontinuity is handled correctly.
