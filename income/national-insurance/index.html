Direct answer

For 2026/27, employee Class 1 NI is 8% on weekly earnings between £242 and £967, then 2% above. Self-employed Class 4 NI is 6% on profits £12,570 to £50,270, then 2% above. Class 2 voluntary NI is £3.65 per week103.

When rates last changed

The employee main rate dropped from 12% to 10% (6 Jan 2024), then to 8% (6 Apr 2024). Class 4 dropped from 9% to 6% on 6 Apr 2024. Class 2 became voluntary above the Small Profits Threshold on 6 Apr 2024. Rates have held since.
