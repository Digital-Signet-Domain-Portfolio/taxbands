UK income tax bands

Direct answer

The UK has four income tax bands for 2026/27 (England, Wales and Northern Ireland). Personal allowance £12,570 (0%)1, basic rate to £50,270 (20%), higher rate to £125,140 (40%), additional rate above £125,140 (45%). The thresholds are frozen to April 203130. Scotland uses a separate six-band regime.

The four bands

  • Personal allowance – £0 to £12,570, taxed at 0%. Withdrawn at £1 for every £2 over £100,0005.
  • Basic rate – £12,570 to £50,270, taxed at 20%.
  • Higher rate – £50,270 to £125,140, taxed at 40%.
  • Additional rate – above £125,140, taxed at 45%.

Dividends, savings and capital gains are taxed at different rates but slot into these bands by sequence. Income tax sits on top of National Insurance, which is itself a banded charge.
