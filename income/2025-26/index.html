Direct answer

For the tax year 2025/26 (6 April 2025 to 5 April 2026), UK-wide bands were: PA £12,570 at 0%, basic to £50,270 at 20%, higher to £125,140 at 40%, additional above £125,140 at 45%4. All four held into 2026/27 unchanged.

Why this matters for back tax

HMRC allows claims for overpaid tax up to four tax years back, so a claim for 2025/26 can be made up to 5 April 2030. Read the 2025/26 PAYE thresholds in the rates-and-thresholds-for-employers sheet4.
