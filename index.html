Direct answer

For 2026/27, UK income tax bands are: personal allowance £12,570 (0%)1, basic rate to £50,270 (20%), higher rate to £125,140 (40%), additional rate above £125,140 (45%). Scotland uses six bands: 19%, 20%, 21%, 42%, 45% and 48%14. The average English Band D council tax is £2,392 for 2026/27, up 4.9% on 2025/2637.

What changed for 2026/27

  • Personal allowance, higher-rate and additional-rate thresholds remain frozen, now to April 203130.
  • The two lower dividend tax rates rose by 2 percentage points on 6 April 2026: basic rate 10.75%, higher 35.75%. The additional rate is unchanged at 39.35%6.
  • Business Asset Disposal Relief rate moved from 14% to 18% from 6 April 20269.
  • Capital gains tax rates of 18% and 24% (set on 30 October 2024) carry into 2026/27. The annual exempt amount is £3,0008.
  • National Insurance Class 1 employee rate stays at 8%/2%; Class 4 self-employed stays at 6%/2%10.
  • The English council tax referendum cap remains 4.99% (2% adult social care + 2.99% core). Most upper-tier councils used it37.
